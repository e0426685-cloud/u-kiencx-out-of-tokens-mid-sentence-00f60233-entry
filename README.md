A make.fun entry for [Out of Tokens, Mid-Sentence](https://make.fun/competitions/u-kiencx-out-of-tokens-mid-sentence-00f60233).

One line, cut off mid-sentence: an AI that ran out of tokens just as it was
about to say something truly brilliant — and we will never know what it was.

`main.py` (standard library only) prints the entry line and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
